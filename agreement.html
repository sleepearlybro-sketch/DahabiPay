DDahabi Pay

Service terms

Dahabi Pay provides a manual request service for exchanging Tether USDT on TRC20 to Money Go USD. Submitting a request does not transfer cryptocurrency, create a wallet, guarantee acceptance, or complete an exchange. A staff member will contact you on WhatsApp with the next steps.

Quotes and completion

A displayed quote remains valid for the stated period. Expired quotes must be refreshed. Completion depends on staff confirmation, availability, accurate customer information, and any checks required for the transaction. Do not send funds until authorized Dahabi Pay staff confirm the instructions directly.

Customer responsibilities

You must provide accurate contact and payout information, control the accounts involved, and comply with applicable laws. Dahabi Pay may cancel requests that cannot be verified or safely completed.
