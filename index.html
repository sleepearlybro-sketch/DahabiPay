Frequently asked questions

What to expect from this manual service.

Does the website receive my USDT?

No. The site records a request only. Staff provide the next steps directly on WhatsApp.

Is the displayed rate guaranteed?

Your quote is locked for 15 minutes. Submit before it expires; otherwise, review a fresh quote.

Which exchange pair is supported?

Only Tether USDT on the TRC20 network to Money Go USD.

How quickly will the team contact me?

During operating hours, staff review requests as soon as possible. A request is not a guarantee of completion.
