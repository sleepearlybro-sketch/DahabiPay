About us

Dahabi Pay is an automated exchanger connecting cryptocurrencies, electronic account balances and African mobile money rails. We exist to make a transfer between those worlds take minutes rather than days, at a rate you can see before you commit.

What we run

  • Automated payouts across 23 currencies and rails, 24 hours a day.
  • Published reserves, refreshed after every completed order, so you know what is available before paying.
  • A locked quote for 20 minutes on every application.
  • Human operators on Telegram for the cases automation should not decide.

How we handle funds

Payout keys are held in cold storage under operator separation; hot wallets carry no more than the published reserve. Both legs of every order are screened before payout. See the AML/KYC Policy for the detail.
