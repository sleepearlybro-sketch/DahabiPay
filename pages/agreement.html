1. Subject

This agreement governs the use of the Dahabi Pay service ("the Service") for exchanging cryptocurrencies, electronic account balances and mobile money. Creating an application constitutes acceptance of this agreement in full.

2. The exchange application

  • An application fixes the direction, amount and rate at the moment of creation and stays valid for 20 minutes.
  • Payment received after the lock expires is recalculated at the rate in force when funds are credited.
  • The customer is solely responsible for the accuracy of the receiving requisite. Completed payouts to an incorrect requisite cannot be recalled.
  • One application accepts one transfer. Split or repeated payments against a single application are processed manually.

3. Fees and rates

The service fee is included in the displayed rate and shown per direction in the rates table. Network and correspondent fees on the sending side are borne by the customer. See Fixing rates for how floating and fixed directions are quoted.

4. Limits and reserves

Minimum and maximum amounts are published per currency and enforced by the exchange form. Orders exceeding the published reserve require prior agreement with an operator.

5. Prohibited use

  • Funds from, or destined for, criminal activity, sanctions evasion or terrorist financing.
  • Exchanges on behalf of a third party without disclosing that fact.
  • Use of the Service where it is unlawful for the customer to do so.

Applications matching these criteria are suspended and handled under the AML/KYC Policy.

6. Liability

The Service is not liable for blockchain congestion, third-party payment system outages, or losses arising from requisites supplied incorrectly by the customer. Liability in any case is limited to the amount of the affected application.

7. Refunds

Refunds are governed by the Returns Policy.

8. Data

Personal data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

This agreement may be amended at any time. The version in force is the one published at the moment an application is created.
