AML / KYC Policy

Template text for a demo build. Obligations differ sharply by jurisdiction - obtain local advice before operating.

Purpose

This policy sets out how Dahabi Pay prevents the Service being used for money laundering, terrorist financing or sanctions evasion, and how customer due diligence is applied.

Risk-based approach

Due diligence scales with risk. Routine exchanges within published limits proceed without documents. Enhanced checks are triggered by:

  • cumulative volume above the published verification threshold within a rolling 30 days;
  • an address or counterparty flagged by our blockchain analytics provider;
  • structuring patterns - repeated amounts just under a limit;
  • requisites in, or routed through, a sanctioned or high-risk jurisdiction;
  • any refund or chargeback request.

Customer due diligence

Where enhanced checks apply, we may request: a government-issued identity document; a selfie holding that document; proof of address dated within three months; proof of source of funds; and confirmation that the sending requisite belongs to the customer.

Screening

  • Crypto addresses on both legs are screened against sanctions and illicit-activity datasets before payout.
  • Customer names are screened against sanctions and PEP lists.
  • Positive matches are escalated to the compliance officer; the application is held, not cancelled, pending review.

Consequences

Where risk cannot be resolved, we may refuse the application and return funds to the originating requisite less network costs, or - where law requires - freeze funds and report to the competent authority. We do not notify the customer where notification is itself prohibited.

Record keeping

Application records, requisites and verification material are retained for the statutory period applicable to the operating entity, then deleted. Access is limited to compliance staff.
