Fixing rates

Floating directions

Most directions are quoted from the live market. The rate you see is recomputed continuously from the mid-market price of each leg plus the service fee for the sending currency.

The 20-minute lock

Creating an application freezes the quote for 20 minutes. If the payment is credited inside that window, you receive exactly the amount shown on the application - whatever the market did in between.

If the window expires before funds are credited, the application is recalculated at the rate in force at the moment of crediting. You may accept the new amount or request a refund under the Returns Policy.

Fixed directions

Directions where both legs are stable - stablecoin to stablecoin, or an internal balance to an electronic account - are quoted at a fixed rate and are not recalculated.

How the cross rate is built

Every currency carries a USD pivot. A cross rate is:

rate = (pivot_send × (1 - fee_send)) ÷ pivot_receive

So the fee is always taken on the sending side, and the receiving amount is what you are quoted. The fee per currency is published in the rates table.

Amount recalculation

  • Underpayment: processed at the received amount if still above the minimum, otherwise refunded.
  • Overpayment: the excess above the published maximum is refunded to the sending requisite.
  • Reserve shortfall at payout time: the order is held and an operator contacts you with the options.
