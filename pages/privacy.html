Privacy Policy

Template text for a demo build.

What we collect

  • Application data - direction, amounts, sending and receiving requisites, timestamps.
  • Contact data - the email address supplied for status notifications, and login credentials for registered accounts.
  • Technical data - IP address, user agent and referrer, for fraud prevention and rate limiting.
  • Verification data - only where the AML/KYC Policy requires it.

Why we process it

To execute the exchange you requested (contractual necessity); to meet anti-money-laundering and record-keeping obligations (legal obligation); and to prevent fraud and abuse (legitimate interest). We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising profiling.

Who we share it with

  • Payment systems and blockchain networks strictly as needed to deliver the payout.
  • Blockchain analytics and sanctions-screening providers.
  • Competent authorities where a lawful request is received.

Retention

Application and verification records are kept for the statutory period applicable to the operating entity. Technical logs are kept for 90 days. Accounts deleted on request are anonymised except where retention is legally required.

Your rights

Subject to local law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to support@dahabipay.example. We respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We set only the cookies and local storage needed for the session, the selected language and the last-used currency pair. No third-party advertising or analytics cookies are set without consent.

Security

Traffic is served over TLS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Payout keys are held in cold storage with operator separation; hot wallet balances are capped at the published reserve.
