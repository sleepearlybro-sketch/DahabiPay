Returns Policy

Template text for a demo build.

When a refund is possible

  • The payout has not yet been executed.
  • Payment arrived after the quote lock expired and the recalculated rate is not acceptable to you.
  • The direction became unavailable - reserve exhausted or payment system outage - before payout.
  • An amount outside the published limits was sent and cannot be processed.

When a refund is not possible

  • The payout has already been executed to the requisite you supplied, including where that requisite was wrong.
  • The refund is requested solely because the market moved after your payment.
  • The application is under compliance hold; funds are released only once that review concludes.

How to request one

  1. Write to support@dahabipay.example within 24 hours, quoting the application ID.
  2. Include the transaction hash or payment reference and the requisite the refund should return to - this must be the requisite the funds came from.
  3. Respond to any verification request; identity confirmation is mandatory before any refund is released.

Costs and timing

Refunds are returned less the network or payment system cost of the return transfer. Decisions are issued within 3 business days; approved refunds are sent within 1 business day of the decision.

Disputes

If you disagree with a decision, reply to the same thread requesting escalation to the compliance officer. Provide any additional evidence at that point.
