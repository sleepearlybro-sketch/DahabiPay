Privacy notice

Dahabi Pay collects the name, verified email, WhatsApp number, Money Go account identifier, exchange amounts, optional note, IP address, and browser metadata needed to review and complete a manual exchange request. Staff use this information to contact you, process the request, prevent abuse, keep an audit record, and meet applicable business obligations.

How data is handled

Request data is stored in our protected Supabase database. Authorized staff access it through the Dahabi Pay operations panel. Money Go details stay inside that panel and are not included in operations notification emails. We do not sell customer data.

Retention and questions

Records are retained while required for operations and applicable legal obligations. Contact support@dahabipay.com with privacy questions or requests.
